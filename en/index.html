About Our Live

An instrumental five-piece — no vocals, no language barrier. The set scales from full festival volume to a pulled-back groove for smaller stages and noise-sensitive time slots. We design the energy arc around your schedule: upbeat daytime sets for afternoon stages, a curated golden-hour set for the 17:00–18:00 window, or a late-night club build. As event organizers ourselves since 2013, we understand turnaround times, sound limits, and crowd flow from the programmer's side.

About
"We screened films by the sea in Zushi and played records after. Kept doing that, and eventually we started making the sounds ourselves."

Half Mile Beach Club is an instrumental band formed in Zushi and based in Tokyo. Since 2013, the group has operated as a cultural collective — organizing film screenings, live shows, and DJ events at the seaside Cinema Amigo in Zushi. The band project grew from that scene: a sound that blends Latin jazz rhythms, club music textures, and indie rock energy, all performed live with no vocals.

Produced by Saito "JxJx" Jun (YOUR SONG IS GOOD), the band went fully instrumental in 2022. The second album Days of the Ocean Waves (2024, P-VINE distribution, also on vinyl) was followed by a remix album (Aug 2025) featuring Paris-based Hugo LX and other international trackmakers.

The core four members cover guitar, bass, drums, and synthesizer, with a guest percussionist rounding out the live lineup to five — with individuals also active as DJs, a trackmaker, and the band's in-house visual designer (all artwork and flyers are made by member Asakura Tsukue).
